Policies

Privacy policy

Draft policy. A Korean version (개인정보 처리방침) will be published alongside it before entries or payments open.

What we collect

  • Opening notice sign-up: name, email, and optionally your website or Instagram and main medium.
  • Enquiries: name or gallery name, email, the package you are interested in, and anything you write in the message.
  • Prize entries: the above, plus images and details of your works, your statement, and country of residence.
  • Payments: handled by our payment provider. We receive confirmation of payment and the payer's name and email, but not your full card details.

Why we use it

  • To send the one opening notice you asked for.
  • To reply to enquiries and deliver services you order.
  • To run the Prize: check eligibility, give jurors access to entries, announce results and pay prizes.
  • To keep records we are required to keep by law, such as payment records.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

  • Tally (tally.so), which hosts our forms and stores what you submit through them.
  • Our payment provider, which processes payments.
  • Jurors, who see entries (works, statement and name) during judging.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this website and may log technical data such as IP addresses.

Some of these providers store data outside Korea.

How long we keep it

  • Opening notice sign-ups: deleted after the notice is sent, or when you ask.
  • Enquiries that do not lead to an order: one year.
  • Prize entries: until the round's results are published plus one year; names and works of shortlisted artists stay in the public archive.
  • Payment and contract records: for the period required by Korean law (generally five years for contracts and payments).

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw consent, at any time by writing to us through the enquiry form. We reply within ten days.

Cookies

This site does not set its own cookies or use analytics. Our form and payment providers may set cookies when you use their pages.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page with the date it takes effect.
