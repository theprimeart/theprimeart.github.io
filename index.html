FAQ

Questions artists ask us

Is this a pay-to-exhibit scheme?

No. The entry fee pays the jurors, the prize and the platform. Being shortlisted or winning costs nothing further, and no paid service affects the jury's decisions.

Who are the jurors and are they really independent?

Each round's jurors are named on this page before the call opens, with their affiliations. They are paid a fixed fee regardless of who enters and abstain from any artist they work with.

What do shortlisted artists actually receive?

An online exhibition of their entered works, an English artist page on The Prime Art with their statement and biography, and inclusion in our announcement to the press and partner galleries.

Can I buy a feature on The Prime Art?

No. Features and prize coverage are editorial. The paid services on this page are labelled and produce deliverables you own; they do not buy editorial coverage.

I'm not based in Korea. Can I enter?

Yes. The prize is open to any nationality. Round 1 focuses its outreach on Korea and Asia, but the jury reviews every entry on the same terms.
