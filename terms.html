Terms and refunds

Draft terms. Business details required by Korean law are shown at the foot of every page once payments open.

1. What these terms cover

These terms apply to the paid services listed on our home page: Korean Press, English Profile & Pitch, Commissioned Essay, and the services for galleries (Launch Sprint, Fair Week Support and Korea Retainer). Entry to the Prize is covered by the separate rules of entry.

2. How an order works

  1. You send an enquiry through our site.
  2. We reply within two working days with a short written scope: what we will deliver, by when, and the price.
  3. You pay using the payment link we send, or the Pay button on our site. Work starts when payment is received and you have sent the materials listed in the scope.
  4. We deliver the files and reports listed in the scope. Everything we deliver is yours to keep.

3. What we do not promise

We are paid for work we do, not for results. We do not promise media coverage, sales, gallery representation, or any particular opinion in a commissioned essay. Commissioned essays are written independently, the critic keeps control of their text, and published essays are labelled "Commissioned essay".

4. Rights

  • Press releases, statements, biographies and translations we write for you: you may use them freely, anywhere, once paid for.
  • Commissioned essays: the critic keeps the copyright. You receive a non-exclusive, permanent licence to reproduce the essay in full, credited to the critic, on your website, in applications, in catalogues and in press materials. Edits beyond factual corrections need the critic's agreement.
  • You confirm you have the right to give us the images and texts you send.

5. Cancellation and refunds

  • Before work starts: full refund if you cancel within 7 days of payment.
  • After work starts: we refund the fee less the value of the work already done, which we will itemise. Once a press release has been distributed, or an essay draft delivered, that part of the work is complete and not refundable.
  • If we cannot deliver what the scope describes, we refund the undelivered part in full.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 7 working days of being agreed.

Nothing in these terms limits your rights under the Korean Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce or other consumer law that applies to you.

6. Prices

Prices on our site include Korean VAT where it applies. Prices for galleries are starting prices; the confirmed price is in your written scope.

7. Contact and disputes

If something goes wrong, write to us through the enquiry form and we will reply within two working days. These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Korea.
