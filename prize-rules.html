The Prime Art Prize · Round 1

Rules of entry

Draft for Round 1. Dates marked TBA are fixed when the call opens. If anything here differs from the published call, the published call applies.

1. Organizer

The Prime Art Prize ("the Prize") is organized by The Prime Art, Seoul ("we"). Business details required by Korean law will be listed at the foot of every page before entries open.

2. Who can enter

  • Artists of any nationality, living anywhere, aged 18 or over on the closing date.
  • Collectives and duos may enter as one artist. One member acts as the contact and receives any prize on the group's behalf.
  • Jurors, their immediate family, and anyone working for The Prime Art may not enter.

3. What you can enter

  • Up to three works in any medium, made within the five years before the closing date.
  • Each work is entered with images (or a video link for time-based work), title, year, medium and dimensions or duration.
  • A short statement of up to 150 words, in English. Statements are not judged on the quality of English.
  • Works must be your own original work. Works made with assistants or fabricators are welcome if you are the author.

4. Dates

  • Jury announced: TBA
  • Call opens: TBA
  • Closing date: TBA, 23:59 Korea Standard Time
  • Results announced: TBA

5. Entry fee

The entry fee is US$30 (₩40,000 when paid in Korea) per entry of up to three works. You may enter once per round. There are no further fees if you are shortlisted or win. Refunds are covered in section 10.

6. Judging

Entries are judged by the three jurors named on our home page, under the process described in How the Prime Art Prize is judged. Jurors abstain from entries where they have a conflict of interest. The organizer does not score entries. The jury's decisions on the work are final.

7. Awards

  • Winner (one artist): ₩3,000,000 cash; a commissioned 1,000-word English essay by an independent critic; a long-form interview published on The Prime Art.
  • Shortlist (twenty artists, including the winner): inclusion in the online exhibition and an English artist page on The Prime Art.
  • Prize money is paid by bank transfer within 30 days of the results, subject to any tax withholding required by Korean law. Bank and transfer fees for overseas payments may be deducted.

8. Your rights in your work

You keep the copyright in your work and your statement. By entering, you allow us to reproduce images of the entered works, with your name credited, for judging, the online exhibition, your artist page, and publicity for the Prize, in any medium, for the current round and in our archive of past rounds. We will not sell your images or use them in paid advertising for anything other than the Prize. You confirm you have the right to grant this, including for any photographs of your work taken by others.

9. Disqualification

We may remove an entry that does not meet these rules, infringes someone else's rights, or was entered with false information. We will write to you first and give you a chance to respond, except where the law requires us to act immediately.

10. Cancellation and refunds

  • If we cancel or postpone the round by more than 60 days, you can have the entry fee refunded in full.
  • You can withdraw your entry for a full refund up to 7 days after payment, provided the closing date has not passed.
  • After that, the fee is not refundable, because judging costs are committed per entry.
  • If your entry is removed under section 9 before judging starts, we refund the fee less payment processing costs.

11. Personal information

We use your details to run the Prize, as described in our privacy policy.

12. Changes and governing law

We may correct errors in these rules or change them where circumstances outside our control require it. We will publish any change and tell artists who have already entered. These rules are governed by the laws of the Republic of Korea.
